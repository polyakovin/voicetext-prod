SPEECH TO TEXT CONVERTER

Set up dictation in Chrome

If you still need the extension, add it to Chrome.

01

Pin the extension

Click the puzzle piece in the top-right corner of Chrome, then click the pin next to Speech to Text Converter.

02

Try dictating

Click the text field below, then click the microphone button in the extension. Allow Chrome to use your microphone and start speaking. You can also dictate without the extension.

03

Use it on other websites

Select a text field on the site and start dictating with the extension. Your words appear in the field and in the side panel. If the words don’t appear in the field, copy them from the side panel and paste them yourself.

When you use browser recognition, the extension saves your history in Chrome. If you use an optional AI feature, it sends only the audio or text you submit.
