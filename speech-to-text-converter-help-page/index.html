Microphone and text fields

If the microphone won't start, check the site's microphone permission in Chrome. In your system settings, make sure Chrome has permission to use the microphone.

If the text doesn't appear in a field on a website, dictate in the extension's side panel. Dictation uses the browser's speech recognition service and may depend on your internet connection.
