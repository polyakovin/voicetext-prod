DATA AND PRIVACY

How voicetext handles your data

For Speech to Text Converter in Chrome, see the extension privacy policy.

Last updated: November 8, 2026

What we save in your browser

Voicetext saves your draft, selected language, last 20 dictations, and voice and speed settings in your browser's localStorage. The reader saves books and reading positions in IndexedDB. This data stays with the current browser and site address. It doesn't sync automatically with the Chrome extension or the original TTS Reader.

You can delete individual dictations and books on the site or clear all site data in your browser. To keep a copy before clearing site data, download your texts as TXT files or export your library as JSON. Private browsing may limit what the browser can save.

Dictation and reading aloud

The site uses your microphone only when you start dictation. Your browser's Web Speech service processes the audio and may send it to its provider. Voicetext doesn't record audio files or send them to its own server.

The reader uses your browser's speech synthesis (speechSynthesis). The available voices depend on your device. Some voices need an internet connection and may send text to their provider.

The tools that transcribe files, make subtitles, and generate WAV files run on your device. They download speech models and the files needed to run them. Your recordings and text stay on your device during that processing.

Files you import

Voicetext reads TXT, JSON, and PDF files in your browser without uploading them. It uses a local PDF.js worker to read PDFs. If a scanned PDF has no selectable text, run optical character recognition (OCR) on it before importing it.

External pages and forms

When you click “Open in Gmail”, the site passes your draft to Gmail. The YouTube and dictation bookmarks run only when you click them on the page where you want to use them. Dictation uses that site's microphone permission and your browser's speech recognition service.

Google Forms hosts the support, feedback, and uninstall forms. Your browser contacts Google when you click “Show form here” or open a form in a new tab. Google receives your answers only when you submit the form. Links to the Chrome Web Store and GitHub also take you off this site.

Offline use

After a successful first visit, a service worker keeps a copy of the site and its local files so you can reopen the reader offline. Speech recognition and online voices may still need an internet connection. Clear the site's data to remove the offline copy.

Analytics and hosting

Voicetext doesn't use product analytics, advertising trackers, or error telemetry. The web host receives standard network requests and may keep technical logs under its own policies.

Questions

Use the support page for questions about data handling.
