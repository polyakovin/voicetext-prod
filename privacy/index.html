DATA AND PRIVACY

Your words. Your control.

This page covers the portal tools. The Speech to Text Converter extension has its own privacy policy.

Last updated: November 6, 2026

Local storage

Your draft, selected language, and last 20 dictations are saved in localStorage. Books and reading positions are saved in IndexedDB; voice and speed preferences are saved in localStorage. This data belongs to the current browser and site address. It does not automatically sync with the extension or the original TTS Reader.

Delete dictations and books in the interface, or clear this site's data in your browser. Download TXT files and export your library as JSON before clearing data. Private browsing may limit storage.

Recognition and speech synthesis

The microphone starts only when you choose to dictate. Audio is processed by your browser's Web Speech service and may be sent to its provider. The portal does not record audio files or send them to its own backend.

Reading aloud uses speechSynthesis. Voices depend on your device, and some voices may send text to an online provider. Browser speech synthesis is not necessarily offline.

Files and library

TXT, JSON, and PDF files are read in your browser. PDF parsing uses a locally bundled PDF.js worker. Files are not uploaded to the portal server. Scanned PDFs without a text layer need OCR before import.

External pages and forms

Support, feedback, and uninstall forms use Google Forms. An embedded form loads only after you click “Load form on this page”. Loading it sends a browser request to Google; answers are sent when you submit the form. Chrome Web Store and GitHub links open external websites.

Offline use

A service worker caches the portal and its local assets so the reader can reopen without a connection after the first successful visit. Browser recognition and online voices may still require internet access. Clear the site's data to remove its offline cache.

Analytics and hosting

This version has no product analytics, advertising trackers, or error telemetry. Hosting receives ordinary network requests and may keep technical logs under its own policies.

Questions

Use the support page for questions about data handling.
